About the Project

CaseBook is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) engineered for Indian advocates, law chambers, and munshis to manage their complete litigation practice — case registers, daily cause lists, hearing schedules, court & helper directories, client dossiers, financial khata, and deadlines — seamlessly online or offline.

Key Practice Modules

  • Home & New Home: Executive KPIs, today's hearings & quick actions
  • Cause List & Upcoming: Daily appearance board & 7-day horizon
  • Calendar & Forward Dates: Monthly scheduler & 1-click date updates
  • Case Registers: All Cases, Undated, Dossier Detail & Disposed archive
  • Paisa Manager: Chambers Khata, fee receipts & WhatsApp closing
  • Courts & Helpers: Judicial directory, readers & munshi contacts
  • To-Do & Transfers: Deadlines, sub-tasks & court transfer audit log
  • Live CRUD & Settings: Supabase cloud sync, CSV export & theme engine

Technology & Architecture

  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties & ES Modules (zero build step)
  • Design System: Advocate Charcoal & Gold token engine (Light & Midnight Dark)
  • Offline Engine: Service Worker (sw.js) + localStorage-first persistence
  • Cloud Sync: Optional Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Quality Gate: Vitest unit suite & Playwright cross-viewport responsive tests
