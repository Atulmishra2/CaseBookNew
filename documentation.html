1. Executive Summary & Practice Mission

CaseBook is a digital legal practice platform engineered specifically for Indian advocates, law chambers, and munshis. It streamlines the day-to-day lifecycle of litigation — from case registration and judicial order tracking to automated daily cause list generation, hearing notifications via WhatsApp, court staff management, task checklists, and khata expense management.

Unlike generic software, CaseBook is structured around authentic Indian procedural law, providing tailored dynamic fields for Civil Suits, Criminal Cases, State/Police Matters, Family Law, Revenue Matters, Miscellaneous Petitions, and Criminal Complaints.

Multi-Jurisdiction Filing

Tailored legal registers for Civil, Criminal, State, Revenue, Family, Misc, and Complaint cases with Indian statutory acts.

Daily Cause List Board

Dynamic court appearance board filtered by date and court. Instant A4 print-ready output with advocate signature blocks.

Accounts & Khata Ledger

Tracks client fee receipts, court work expenses (certified copies, stamps, clerkage), net savings margin, and WhatsApp closing.

Offline-First Cloud Sync

Operates uninterrupted offline via Service Worker (sw.js) and SafeStorage, with two-way sync to Supabase PostgreSQL.

2. Architecture & Technical Foundations

CaseBook is designed with a zero-build-step, modular vanilla web architecture. Every tab lives in its own isolated folder under components/tabs/ with scoped CSS and dedicated JavaScript, ensuring fast load times, zero framework rot, and complete offline autonomy.

Layer Modules & Files Purpose & Characteristics
App Shell & Design System index.html, styles/tokens.css, styles/layout.css, DESIGN.md Advocate Charcoal & Gold token engine supporting Light and Midnight Dark modes ([data-theme="dark"]) with strict tab isolation.
Service Modules services/caseService.js, todoService.js, ancillaryService.js, supabaseClient.js Clean separation of concerns for case CRUD, task checklists, courts/helpers, khata accounts, and Supabase integration.
Utilities & Custom UI components/custom-calendar.js, utils/caseNumberUtils.js, dateUtils.js, exportUtils.js, uiUtils.js Custom Monday-first date picker, case number normalization, CSV exports, accessible tooltips, and safe storage wrappers.
Offline Resilience (PWA) sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, serve.json Service Worker caching and localStorage-first persistence so chambers can operate without internet inside court complexes.
Cloud Backend supabase_schema/*.sql (PostgreSQL + RLS) Normalized tables for specialized case types, unified views, hearings, accounts, courts, helpers, and Row Level Security hardening.
Testing & QA vitest.config.js, playwright.config.mjs, tests/ Unit tests via Vitest (jsdom) and cross-viewport responsive audits via Playwright (desktop 1280px & mobile 392px/412px).

3. User Roles & Access Hierarchy

CaseBook strictly separates administrative chamber management (index.html + admin.js) from public/client case tracking (login.html):

1. Admin Console (Chambers Advocate & Staff) Full read/write/delete privileges across all cases, hearing forward dates, courts directory, financial ledger, and cloud synchronization. Access is guarded by session authentication in admin.js.
2. Client Guest Tracking Portal Read-only portal allowing litigants and clients to look up next hearing dates, court venues, and case status using their Case Number or Mobile Number. Confidential chamber notes, finances, and deletion actions are strictly concealed.

4. Operational Guide by Module

4.1 Home & New Home Dashboards

The Home Dashboard (and Zen New Home view) serves as the morning command center, summarizing practice health with live KPI counters:

  • Total Active Matters: Overall pending litigation across all jurisdictions.
  • Listed Today: Real-time count of cases listed on the current calendar date across all courts.
  • Upcoming (7 Days): Hearings scheduled over the next 7 days.
  • Disposed / Decided: Historical archive of completed matters.

4.2 Dynamic Case Registration & Case Operations

Case registration (add / manage tabs) dynamically adapts its input fields based on the selected Case Category:

  • Civil Cases: Plaintiff vs Defendant, Suit Value (₹), Court Fees paid, Act & Section (CPC, Specific Relief, Succession), Process Stage.
  • Criminal & State Cases: State/Complainant vs Accused, Police Station, Crime/FIR Number, Penal Sections (BNS / IPC, BNSS / CrPC, NDPS, POCSO), Bail Status & Surety.
  • Family Disputes: Petitioner vs Respondent, Marriage Date, Conciliation / Mediation Stage, Maintenance orders.
  • Revenue & Misc / Complaint Cases: Land village, Khasra/Gata Number, SDM / Tehsildar / Revenue Board forum, or NI Act / complaint details.

4.3 Cases Register, Undated, Detail & Disposed

The Cases Register (all) houses the master case inventory with table and card views, alongside Undated Cases (undated), full Case Details dossier (detail), and Disposed Cases (disposed):

Instant Search → Court & Type Filter → Case Dossier → WhatsApp Notice

4.4 Daily Cause List & Appearance Board

Generates the daily court board (causelist) for the advocate and junior team with 5-column sorting and expandable docket rows synchronized with Upcoming and Calendar views:

  • Appearance Date Selector: Select any date using the custom calendar or quick preset pills.
  • Court Filter: Filter the day's cause list by specific court or view the consolidated chambers list.
  • Print Daily Cause List: Generates a formal A4 printable document (components/print/print-templates.html) with advocate seal and signature block.
  • WhatsApp & Pass-Over Slip: Send client updates or generate pass-over slips directly from each docket row.

4.5 Court Hearing Calendar & Forward Dates

Visual monthly grid (calendar) with appearance counts per day. Clicking any date reveals all scheduled matters. The Forward Dates tab (hearing) allows rapid date assignment:

  • Enter Case Number or select from the auto-complete / find-and-replace list.
  • Select the next hearing date and the next judicial stage (e.g. Evidence, Arguments, Framing of Issues, Order).
  • Record judicial remarks and automatically update the case record, hearing timeline, and next cause list.

4.6 Paisa Manager (Chambers Accounts & Khata)

A purpose-built legal accounting module (paisa) tracking daily client receipts and disbursements:

Nature Description & Typical Categories Financial Effect
Work + Expense Client fee received plus associated court work expenditure (certified copies, stamps, clerkage, typing). Inflow (+) and Outflow (-); calculates exact Net Chamber Savings live.
Direct Client Fee Pure advocate professional consultation fees, brief retention, or appearance fee. 100% Inflow (+); increments chamber net savings directly.
Chamber Expense General practice overheads: stationery, photostat paper, process fees, and travel. 100% Outflow (-); deducted from net earnings.

Features dual view modes (Visual Cards and Spreadsheet Table), CSV Export, A4 Printable Khata Ledger, and Daily Closing on WhatsApp.

4.7 Court & Helpers Directory

Manages judicial establishments (District Courts, Commercial Courts, Family Courts, High Court Benches) in courts and court staff contacts (Readers, Ahlmads, Peons, Munshis) in helpers with direct phone dialers and WhatsApp integration.

4.8 Task Deadlines & To-Do Board

Tracks urgent legal filing deadlines, limitation dates, affidavit preparations, and certified copy follow-ups in todo, including priority tagging and checklist sub-steps.

4.9 Case Transfer Management

Maintains an immutable audit trail when cases are transferred between courts, benches, or districts via transfer, supporting single-case and bulk migrations.

4.10 Live CRUD & Supabase Cloud Synchronization

Provides two-way data replication between local chamber devices and Supabase PostgreSQL via livecrud and settings (DB Studio), with live connection status and manual sync triggers.

6. Security, Backups & Maintenance

Recommended Daily Practice Routine
  1. Morning: Open My Cause List → Print / Save PDF of the day's court appearance board.
  2. During Court Hours: Record certified copy expenses, stamps, and fee receipts in Paisa Manager.
  3. Evening: Open Forward Dates to log the next hearing dates; click Daily Closing on WhatsApp to review the day's financial summary.
  4. Weekly: Open Settings & Cloud → export CSV/JSON backup of cases and accounts ledger.
